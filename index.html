How it works

1. Start with the athlete

We assess movement patterns, current confidence, and age-appropriate needs to meet each athlete where they are.

2. Train the full system

Sessions blend movement skill, creative problem-solving, and nervous system regulation so athletes can perform with composure.

3. Build lasting growth

Families receive clear progress guidance, home support tools, and a pathway that grows with the child over time.

Age pathways

Ages 6–9

Foundation

Movement literacy, body awareness, and playful confidence through guided, engaging sessions.

Ages 10–13

Development

Coordination, creativity under pressure, and composure habits that transfer to sport and school.

Ages 14–18

Performance

Advanced athletic development, confidence in competition, and leadership through intentional training.

What families get

  • High-quality coaching in a supportive, growth-focused environment.
  • A structured training pathway tailored to age and development stage.
  • Tools for stress regulation and composure before, during, and after competition.
  • Clear communication, progress markers, and practical at-home guidance.
  • An experience that prioritizes long-term human development over quick fixes.

Our philosophy

Great athletes are developed, not rushed. We believe the strongest competitive edge comes from mastering movement, regulating the nervous system, and developing confidence that holds up under pressure.

At Player One Athletics, performance and personal growth are connected. When young athletes learn to move well, think creatively, and stay composed, they carry those skills into sport, school, and life.
